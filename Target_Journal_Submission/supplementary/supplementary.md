# S1. Datasets
Table S1 lists all {{N_DATASETS}} datasets (name, task, size, source, license) and the per-dataset
data-integrity audit: exact-duplicate groups by standardization-invariant canonical identity, label
disagreement among duplicates, standardization-collapse pairs (distinct raw SMILES mapping to one
molecule), scaffold-split canonical-identity leakage (0 by construction), and train/test
near-duplicate (Tanimoto ≥ 0.95) rate. Generated by `scripts/leakage_audit.py`.

# S2. Standardization pipelines and the orbit
Table S-pipelines lists the six named pipelines and the five operation axes. Tautomers are ranked by
RDKit's tautomer-plausibility score (ScoreRings + ScoreSubstructs + ScoreHeteroHs) and the top forms
retained; the enumerator is bounded (max 64 tautomers / 80 transforms) for tractability without
changing the retained top-scored forms. Protonation at pH 7.4 uses Dimorphite-DL.

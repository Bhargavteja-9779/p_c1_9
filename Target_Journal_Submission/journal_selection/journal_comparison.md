## Decision
Ten+ Q1 candidates were internally evaluated (Elsevier KBS/ESWA/Information Sciences/EAAI/Pattern Recognition/Neurocomputing/Applied Soft Computing; Springer DMKD/Machine Learning/AI Review/Complex & Intelligent Systems; OUP Briefings/Bioinformatics/JCDE; IEEE TNNLS/TAI; Wiley IJIS; IET CAAI TIT). Elsevier/IEEE AI venues with high JIF were rejected on **timeline** (verified median submission→acceptance >140 days for KBS/ESWA/Information Sciences; Elsevier/IEEE do not deposit received/accepted dates to Crossref/PubMed, and publisher-reported "submission to acceptance" for ESWA is 147–187 d — all **OVER the 100-day limit**). Springer AI venues (DMKD median 245 d, Machine Learning 208 d, Neural Computing & Applications 254 d) are far over the limit. Among venues with (a) verified median acceptance ≤100 days, (b) genuine fit for molecular-ML *methodology*, and (c) Q1 JCR status, **JCIM** is the strongest: median 84 d, Q1, and a demonstrated appetite for exactly this work. Journal of Cheminformatics is an excellent scientific fit but its census median (132 d) exceeds the target, and it is gold-OA-only; it is the primary backup.
